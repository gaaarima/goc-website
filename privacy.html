Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect the personal information entrusted to us.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Gandaki Overseas Concern Pvt. Ltd. (“GOC”, “we”, “us”) is a recruitment company licensed by the Government of Nepal (Licence No. 115/055/056). We handle personal information of job seekers, employers and visitors to this website. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the laws of Nepal, including the Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018), and the rules that govern foreign employment.

1. Information collected through this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not have user accounts.

  • Enquiry form. The “Send an Enquiry” form does not send anything to a server. When you press Send Enquiry, it opens a pre-filled email in your own email app. Nothing reaches us unless you send that email. If you do, we receive your name or company, your email address, the country of employment and your message.
  • Email and phone. If you email or call us, we receive the details you choose to share.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. This is handled under GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Fonts. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy.

2. Job seekers and candidates

To find, assess and deploy workers for overseas employers, we collect information from candidates directly, at interviews or through our data bank of applicants. This may include:

  • name, contact details, date of birth, nationality and photographs
  • passport and citizenship details
  • CV, education, qualifications, work history, skills and references
  • results of trade tests and interviews
  • medical fitness reports and police clearance, where an employer or destination country requires them
  • visa, labour approval, insurance and travel details needed for deployment

We use this information to match candidates with suitable jobs, prepare and forward CVs to employers, obtain visas and labour approvals, arrange travel and deployment, and meet our legal duties as a licensed recruitment agency. We only ask for medical or criminal-record information when the job or the law requires it.

3. Employers and clients

From employers, we collect business contact details and the documents needed to recruit legally from Nepal. These include the job order (demand letter), power of attorney, service agreement, guarantee letter, consular letter and employment contract, along with the names and contact details of the people we deal with. We use this to provide our recruitment services, obtain prior approval from the Department of Labour, and keep proper records.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only when needed to complete a recruitment or when the law requires it, with:

  • Employers considering a candidate, who receive the candidate’s CV and supporting documents
  • Government authorities in Nepal, including the Department of Labour and Employment Promotion, for approvals and records
  • Embassies and consulates of the destination country, for visas
  • Approved medical centres, insurers, airlines and travel agents involved in a candidate’s deployment
  • Anyone else the law requires us to disclose information to, such as a court or regulator

Because we recruit for employers abroad, candidate information is sent to the destination country, for example the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Saudi Arabia or Malaysia. We share only what the employer and the authorities need for that recruitment.

5. Security and how long we keep information

We keep paper and electronic records secure and limit access to staff who need them for their work. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the recruitment it was collected for, to support candidates during employment, and to meet the record-keeping requirements of Nepali law. After that, we securely destroy or delete it. Candidates in our data bank can ask us to remove their details at any time.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date
  • delete your information, or remove you from our candidate data bank, unless we must keep it by law or for an ongoing recruitment
  • stop contacting you about job opportunities

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

7. Children

We recruit only people who meet the legal minimum age for foreign employment, and this website is not intended for children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
