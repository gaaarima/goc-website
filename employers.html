For Employers

Legal documents required to begin recruitment from Nepal.

Documents Required

Under the Foreign Employment Act of the Government of Nepal, recruitment is formal and legal only with prior approval from the Labour Department. To obtain that approval, the employer must provide the following documents.

  • Job Order (Demand Letter)
  • Power of Attorney
  • Service Agreement
  • Guarantee Letter
  • Consular Letter
  • Specimen Employment Contract
Download PDF Checklist ↓

Attestation Requirements

All documents must be on company letterhead, duly signed by the authorised person and bearing the company seal. They are accepted by the Labour Department in Nepal only when attested by both:

  1. The Chamber of Commerce and Industry of the employment country
  2. The Embassy of Nepal located in the employment country

GOC guides every client through this documentation step-by-step — most employers complete it within the standard lead time.
