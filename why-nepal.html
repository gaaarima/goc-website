Frequently Asked Questions

What employers usually ask us before hiring from Nepal.

Is GOC a licensed recruitment agency?
Yes. Gandaki Overseas Concern Pvt. Ltd. has operated under Government of Nepal Licence No. 115/055/056 since 1998 (CR No. 9177/055/56), and is an ISO 9001 certified company — you can view the certificate by clicking the ISO 9001 badge at the top of any page.
Which countries do you supply workers to?
We currently deploy workers to the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Saudi Arabia and Malaysia — over 120 client companies across six countries. See our clients for the full list.
What categories of workers can you provide?
Five major fields: construction (from civil, electrical, mechanical, architectural and survey engineers down to general labour), hospitality, security and facility services, manufacturing, and administration & management. The full list is on the workforce page.
How long does the recruitment process take?
Forwarding of documents takes a minimum of one week to a maximum of 15 days, depending on the volume and category of the requirement. Deployment takes 10 to 30 days — on-time visa approval plays a vital role in timely deployment.
What documents do we need to provide as an employer?
Six documents on your company letterhead, signed and sealed: a Job Order (demand letter), Power of Attorney, Service Agreement, Guarantee Letter, Consular Letter and a Specimen Employment Contract — attested by your country's Chamber of Commerce and the Nepal Embassy there. Details are on the For Employers page, and we guide you through every step.
How are candidates selected and screened?
All candidates are thoroughly assessed and screened, with records, experience and references validated. Selection is based purely on merit, qualification and the ability to perform the task — never on anything else. Final selection is through interview, by you or on your behalf.
Why choose Nepali workers over other source countries?
Employers tell us the same things again and again: Nepali workers are honest, hardworking and punctual, loyal to their employer, flexible across roles, dedicated even in adverse situations — and relatively cost-effective compared to other source countries.
